    Where takings slip away, and how Occupy stops it

    Most of what goes missing in a bar or a lodge goes missing quietly. Occupy puts a name and a number on it.

    Drinks go out that were never rung up.

    Stock counts down with every sale. Count the shelf and Occupy shows what’s missing, in bottles and in shillings.

    The drawer is short at closing.

    The cash-up shows what should be in the drawer for the shift, cash, card and mobile money apart, against what was counted.

    A tab walks out the door.

    Nothing is rung up without a table or a room, and every open bill stays on the front desk until it’s paid.

    A sale is cancelled after the customer paid.

    Everyone signs in with their own PIN, so every sale and every refund carries a name.

    Before you start

    How much does it cost?

    One price per shop, with every feature included, paid in shillings. Message us for this year’s prices. The trial is free either way.

    What do I need to buy?

    Nothing to start. Occupy runs in the browser on a tablet, phone or laptop. Add a receipt printer when you want printed bills.

    What if the internet goes off?

    Keep selling. Every sale saves on the tablet first, then goes up to the cloud and out to your other tablets when the connection comes back.

    Can I check the shop when I’m not there?

    Yes. Sign in on your phone to see today’s sales, open bills and the cash-up as they happen.

    Can I sell in shillings and pay suppliers in dollars?

    Yes. Price the menu in TSh, KSh or USh, pay suppliers in dollars, and Occupy converts at the rate you set.

    What happens when the trial ends?

    Pick a plan and carry on. If you don’t, the till stops opening, and your menu and sales records are kept for when you come back.
